## 🛰️ ABOUT ME

I am a **Surveying and Geoinformatics graduate from Nigeria** with a strong interest in applying geospatial technologies to understand the Earth, analyse spatial patterns and support evidence-based decision-making.

My work combines:

**Surveying + GIS + Remote Sensing + Earth Observation + Spatial Data Science + Machine Learning**

I have practical experience working with **GNSS/DGPS, Total Stations, UAVs, satellite imagery, GIS platforms, environmental datasets and spatial modelling workflows**.

My interests include **environmental monitoring, digital soil mapping, land-use/land-cover analysis, coastal hazards, urban development, infrastructure surveying, hydrographic surveying and geospatial data science**.
